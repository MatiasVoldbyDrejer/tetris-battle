Its hands

The game's hard computer player. A piece every quarter second, built for tetrises. It will hammer you.

Its head

In any terminal

Play in the terminal

Same game, no Claude required. Alone, against friends on the same network, or against a computer player that does not get tired.

Friends on the same Wi-Fi

One of you hosts

npx tetris-battle host

Everyone else joins

npx tetris-battle join

It finds the host by broadcast. The host presses enter to start, and again for the next match.

Network blocks broadcast? Join by address

npx tetris-battle join 192.168.1.23

Use the address the host's screen shows.

A computer player

Levels easy, normal and hard. Hard builds for tetrises and sends three times the garbage. Take it on alone, or gang up on it with friends.

npx tetris-battle host --agent hard

Alone

Seven-bag dealing, SRS rotation with wall kicks, a ghost piece, hold, and a level every ten lines.

npx tetris-battle

Or keep it, as the tetris command

npm install -g tetris-battle
  • ←→ move
  • ↑ or x rotate
  • ↓ soft drop
  • space hard drop
  • c hold
  • q quit

--name says who you are. The host's --minutes sets how long a match is, and --port moves the game when the usual port is taken.

The rules

Tetris Battle rules

Everyone plays their own game. Clearing lines sends garbage to an opponent: grey rows, one column open, rising under their stack.

ClearLines sent
DoubleA single sends nothing.1
Triple2
Tetris4
T-spin doubleA T-spin single sends 2, a triple 6.4
Back to backA tetris or T-spin straight after another.+1
ComboLocks that each clear a line, one after another: +0, +0, +1, +1, +1, +2 …more
Perfect clearNothing left on the board.+10
on the clock

  • Attack is defence. Lines you send cancel the garbage waiting for you first.

  • Top out, and it's a KO for whoever last hit you. Your board starts over, empty.

  • Two minutes. Most KOs wins, then most lines sent.

The look

Arcade, in a terminal

Every cell is a painted background, which a terminal fills edge to edge, so the board lines up exactly everywhere.

HOLD
KO 1 · sent 12
NEXT

B2B · Double · Combo ×3

  • Bevelled tiles, lighter on the top left, darker on the bottom right.

  • Hold box on the left of the well, the next five pieces on the right.

  • A big block-letter clock across the top.

  • Cleared rows flash before they go. Hard drops leave a trail.

  • Incoming garbage climbs the left wall of your well in red, and shakes the screen when it lands.

  • Shouts in big block letters under your well: TETRIS, T SPIN, with “B2B · Double · Combo ×3” in small print.

  • A board that tops out gets a KO!
